WHAT IS AND IS NOT ESTABLISHED
------------------------------
ESTABLISHED by the user's statement:
- Real audio was audible on device 6.
- To the user's ear it was not obviously wrong (not broken up, not silent, no
  obvious glitching was reported).

NOT ESTABLISHED, and not claimed:
- That the audio content was the CORRECT content. Nothing here showed which
  sounds were audible, whether the track/mix was right, or whether every one of
  the 742 played events was actually heard. "Sounds normal" is a coarse
  judgement, not a content check.
- That the audible result matches the demo's own soundtrack. No reference
  listening pass was done against a known-good player.
- Anything about timing/latency correctness.
- Anything about devices other than 6.
- That audibility holds under load. Note for honesty: during the gate run this
  machine also hosted another task's process (viewmodel_contract_probe.exe,
  PID 12788), and the listening playback overlapped with no other audio job of
  this task's own; a glitch caused by external load would not have been detected
  by the counters, and "sounds normal" was reported once, not repeatedly.

The counters in device-runs.txt describe what the program did. This file
describes what a person heard. Neither one back-certifies the other.
