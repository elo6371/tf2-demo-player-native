WHY THE TWO RUNS ARE RECORDED SEPARATELY
----------------------------------------
T7 step 6 asks for the null-sink and device checks to be run and recorded
separately, and T7 acceptance asks that the null sink not open a device while
device runs use a pinned device id. The two runs below differ in exactly one
flag (--audio-disabled) and are two separate process invocations writing two
separate trace files, so neither reading can be inherited from the other.

THE PINNED DEVICE
-----------------
Device 6 is named explicitly on every device run. The gate refuses the whole
device section when device_count < 7, so an out-of-range id is a FAIL and not a
fallback to the default:

  device=6 query=0 name=耳机 (xduoo audio)          [gate/winmm-devices.txt]
  device_count=13

WAVE_MAPPER is never used: --audio-device 6 is passed on both runs, and the
probe's dry run reports device=0 device_opened=0 device_open_attempts=0
[a] plus the forward player run asserting --audio-disabled opened no device [b].

WHY THESE COUNTERS ARE NOT EVIDENCE OF AUDIBILITY
-------------------------------------------------
Every reading above is a product-side counter. None of them can tell whether a
human heard anything, and none is used as if it could: the human confirmation is
recorded separately in listening.txt and references these numbers only as a
description of what the program did, never as support for what was heard.
