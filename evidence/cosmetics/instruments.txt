# POV 60162: the same demo, the same tick, two instruments, two numbers

Task-40 measured the demo with a probe table; task-41 measured it with a product frame
table. The two examined counts differ (17 vs 37) and the card
(`docs/COSMETICS-2026-11-22.md` section 6) refuses to average them, because they are not
the same instrument. This file puts the two lines one under the other so the disagreement
is visible in one place.

Why the examined counts differ: the probe table walks the entity snapshot queue for the
resolved tick and counts every entity whose class is in the wearable allow-table; the product
frame table counts the rows the render path itself built during that frame, which includes
rows the probe's tick resolution never reached and excludes rows outside the frame's candidate
set. Neither is a superset of the other, which is exactly why the card cites each number with
its own source instead of quoting one as "the" count.

What the two instruments agree on: `renderable` is 2 in the product table and 3 in the probe
table, and both tables put the world-path result at 2 (`draw_path 2 1` = 2 world + 1 viewmodel
in the probe; `worldRenderable=2` in the product line). Both agree that nothing could be
placed this tick -- the product line says why (`noOwnerBody=2`).
