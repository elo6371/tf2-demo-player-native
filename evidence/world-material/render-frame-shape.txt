## What moved, and why

- The fallback capture is byte-identical across the two rounds and four runs
  (sha256 `fa1bb194...`, 139121 colours each time; the gate's own log has quoted
  139121 since 2026-10-08). T3 did not touch the fallback path.
- The installed-map capture moved exactly where the feature moved it: 563 -> 15909
  distinct colours (28x) on the same scene, as per-face lightmaps turned flat faces
  into gradients. `render-frame-installed-map-pre-t3.png` and
  `render-frame-installed-map-t3.png` beside this file are the two frames.
- The paused capture moved too, and the attribution is measured, not guessed: the
  paused run draws a real world. A traced paused capture (2026-10-13) reads
  `gpu: world lightmap status=active intensity=0.479 ... vertices=541665
  lightmapped=169428` and `gpu: world cubemap ... source=materials/maps/2koth_abbey/...`
  -- with no demo the program loads koth_abbey. 169428 = 3 x 56476, and
  `world_material_probe --bsp 2koth_abbey.bsp` reads `lightmapPackedFaces=18854` of
  `lightmapFaces=22991` (4137 faces dropped by the 2048x2048 atlas packer) and
  `trianglesWithLightUv=56476` of 180555, so the parse side and the GPU side agree
  exactly. The paused frame's 180 -> 4419 colours follow from that.
- The old criterion asserted `distinct colours < 1000` on the installed-map frame.
  Its margin was 858x when written (139121/162, 2026-10-08) and 247x when this round
  started (139121/563); the feature moved it to 8.7x, and every further realism step
  (albedo textures are next) moves the world's count toward the decal's with no
  ceiling. The replacement compares the installed-map capture against the fallback
  capture taken in the same run and requires them to differ in >= 10% of payload
  bytes -- an intrinsic property of "the world vs the fallback picture" that does
  not shrink as the world gets prettier.
- The gate's own verdict logs for the two modes on the final script bytes:
  `.scratch/fc-run/straight-verdict-final.txt` and
  `.scratch/fc-run/mutation-verdict-final.txt`; the committed tier record is under
  `verify-render/`.

## The tool bug this record sits next to, and why it is written down

The first version of the scratch loader swapped channels with
`r[0::3], r[1::3] = r[2::3], r[1::3]`, which writes R into both the R slot and the
B slot and drops B. It read 5957 colours where the correct count is 15909 (and 477
where the gate's own counter read 563 on the pre-T3 frame). The disagreement
between two independently written counters on the same bytes is what exposed it,
and the numbers above are the corrected ones. A lossy transform applied to both
sides of a comparison can hide a difference instead of measuring it -- which is
also why the gate's comparison applies no transform at all.
